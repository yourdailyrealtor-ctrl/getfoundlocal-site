FAQ

Common questions

Do I really own the website?+

Yes — fully. The domain is registered in your name, and all files are yours. We'll give you a copy any time you ask. The monthly plan covers hosting and maintenance, not ownership.

Do I have to pay before seeing the site?+

No. We build your site first and send you a preview. You don't pay until you've seen it and approved it. If you hate it, you owe nothing.

What if I want to cancel the monthly plan?+

Cancel anytime, no contracts. Your domain will eventually expire (you'd get advance notice), but we'll send you all your files so you keep the site forever.

How long does it take?+

Usually 3–4 days from "yes" to a live site with your own domain. Most agencies take 4–6 weeks. We move fast.

Can I provide my own photos?+

Absolutely — just send them over and we'll use them. We can also pull photos from your Google Maps listing if that's easier.
