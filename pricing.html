FAQ

Common questions

Do I really own the website?+

Yes — fully. The domain is in your name and all files are yours. We'll send them to you any time you ask. The monthly plan covers hosting and maintenance, not ownership.

Do I pay before seeing the site?+

No. We build it first and send you a preview. You don't pay until you've seen it and approved it.

What if I want to cancel the monthly plan?+

Cancel anytime, no contracts. Your domain will eventually expire (you get advance notice), but we send you all your files so you keep the site.

How long does it take?+

Usually 3–4 days from approval to a live site with your own domain. Most agencies take 4–6 weeks.

Can I send my own photos?+

Absolutely — just email them over. We can also pull from your Google listing if that's easier.
